## What changes

### Changed

- **A quieter close button in modals** (#294): the X at the top right of Settings, the environment editor, the import wizard and every other modal no longer sits on a gray square. It is a light gray X that shows its background when the pointer is over it, so the title stands out. Its tooltip shows `Esc`, which closes the modal too, and reaching it with `Tab` draws a ring around it.
- **Long modal titles wrap** (#294): a title too long for its modal continues on a second line instead of being cut.
