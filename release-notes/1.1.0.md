## What changes

### Changed

- **A quieter close button in modals** (#294): the X at the top right of Settings, the environment editor, the import wizard and every other modal no longer sits on a gray square. It is a light gray X that shows its background when the pointer is over it, so the title stands out. Its tooltip shows `Esc`, which closes the modal too, and reaching it with `Tab` draws a ring around it.
- **Long modal titles wrap** (#294): a title too long for its modal continues on a second line instead of being cut.
- **Inputs that stand out** (#295): every text field, the URL bar and the sidebar filter have a stronger border, a lighter one under the pointer, and a soft accent halo while you type in them, so a field no longer gets lost on its panel.
- **A more compact find bar** (#295): in the request body, the scripts, the Docs tab and the response body, the match counter moves inside the field and **Find in selection** becomes one more toggle, `Alt+L`. The tooltips show their keys. With no match the field turns red and the arrows and the Replace buttons are disabled, and a regular expression that is not valid says what is wrong and where. The selected match has an outline, so it is easy to follow while jumping.
- **More find shortcuts** (#295): `F3` and `Shift+F3` repeat the last search with the find bar closed, `Alt+Up` and `Alt+Down` in the find and replace fields go through the searches of the session, `Ctrl+Alt+Enter` replaces every match and `Alt+P` keeps the case of each match.
