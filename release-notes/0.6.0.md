Postino 0.6.0 is in progress. These notes grow with every change of the milestone and become the GitHub release notes.

## What changes

- **What's new** (#189): after an update, Postino opens a tab with the notes of the new version. Turn it off in Settings, Appearance, and open it at any time with **Show what's new** in the command palette or from About.
- **Git log keys** (#188): after a click on a commit, the arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End move through the history and show each commit's detail.
